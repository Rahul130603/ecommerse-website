Our story

Built by players, for players

Apex Sports began because buying genuine gear in India was unnecessarily hard. Bats arrived with the wrong weight, boots were counterfeit, and nobody could tell you whether a shoe actually suited your gait.

So we built the shop we wished existed. Every product line is tested by someone on our team who plays that sport. Every listing shows the real specifications, the real weight, and the real size guidance. And if something does not fit, we fix it with a free exchange rather than an argument.

How we work

Three rules we do not break

Rule 01

Show the real spec

If a bat weighs 1180 grams, we say 1180 grams. If a shoe runs half a size small, we say that too. No padded marketing numbers.

Rule 02

Never sell a fake

We would rather be out of stock than ship a counterfeit. Every batch is checked in, and every invoice carries our GSTIN.

Rule 03

Fix problems fast

Wrong size, damaged in transit, missing item — one message and we resolve it. No lengthy forms, no blame games.
